# My First Words — iPhone / iPad release preparation

**Current status:** Working web/PWA prototype, not yet a compiled or App Store-approved native iOS app.

## What is ready in this repository
- 20 bilingual picture-word games (80 prompts)
- Browser speech synthesis and touch interactions
- Installable PWA manifest and basic offline caching
- Explicit, visible mobile welcome-screen navigation

## Important outstanding items
1. Supply the owner's chosen horizontal cover illustration (current `cover-art.svg` is a placeholder). Verify licensing and original-asset rights.
2. Test each game on physical Android and iOS devices, including English and Spanish pronunciation, mute, audio playback, rotation and accessibility.
3. Audit and correct any age-inappropriate vocabulary or behavior with a qualified human reviewer.
4. Produce a customer support contact, privacy policy, and accurate data-practices disclosures.
5. Choose a native packaging approach for iOS and generate an Xcode project using a Mac with Xcode. This repository alone is **not** an IPA file.
6. Prepare iPhone/iPad screenshots, production icons (including required Apple sizes), app description, categories, age ratings, and store metadata.
7. If offered as a paid download, configure the price in App Store Connect and keep purchases/external links out of the child-facing experience.
8. Complete TestFlight testing and Apple review. Do not claim Apple approval until received.

## Important
The $4.99 list price is a business proposal, not an active charge. Apple Developer Program membership is separate from the sale price. GitHub Pages may be useful for reviewing the demo but should not be treated as the paid app distribution platform.
