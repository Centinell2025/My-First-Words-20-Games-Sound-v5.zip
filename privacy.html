Privacy Policy — My First Words Kids

Beacon of the Eagle LLC provides My First Words Kids, an educational app designed for young children to use with a parent or caregiver.

The app does not ask children to create an account or provide their name, email address, or other contact details. It stores game progress and, where available, settings on the device. Parents or caregivers can remove this information by clearing the app’s data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

The app uses the device’s text-to-speech service to play preset words and phrases. It does not use the microphone to record a child’s voice. The device’s text-to-speech provider may process information according to its own policies.

We did not identify advertising, analytics, or tracking software in the app source reviewed. Google Play and device service providers may process information under their own privacy policies when the app is distributed or used.

For privacy questions, contact Beacon of the Eagle LLC at beaconoftheeagle@centinellsuitedefence.com.
